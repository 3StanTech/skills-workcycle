# Brand boards and logo systems

Identity boards and logo concepts from a brand brief. Match the requested board format;
example panel grids are options, not extra deliverables. Scope and approval belong to
[medium and providers](medium-and-providers.md).

**Medium:** draw logo marks, construction diagrams, palettes and type specimens as
SVG/HTML (exact and editable). Use an image model only for atmosphere panels
(photography, textures, campaign imagery), then compose the board in HTML so text stays
crisp. A complex illustrative mark may start from an image model and be redrawn as SVG.

## 1. Strategy before visuals

Infer and state: category, audience, product function, emotional promise, cultural
position, trust level, visual world, core metaphor, and what the brand must avoid.
Every board answers: what the brand represents, its metaphor, how the mark expresses it,
how the system scales (UI, print, image, detail), and why it feels ownable.

## 2. Logo

Simple, memorable, symbolic, scalable, usable as icon, wordmark, badge and pattern.
Use one concept method, or two at most:

- **Monogram + meaning:** the initial shaped by a metaphor (cuts, folds, negative space).
- **Product action:** the main action abstracted (build → frame, protect → boundary,
  speak → waveform).
- **Metaphor fusion:** two ideas reduced into one readable mark.
- **Negative space:** a hidden arrow, protected center, cutout initial.
- **Construction geometry:** circles, diagonals, grids, modular blocks; one panel may
  show the construction.

Avoid generic bolts, random animals, fake crests, clipart, meaningless sparkles, copied
famous marks, inconsistent variants. For an existing client brand, never redraw or
replace their real logo; use the supplied file.

## 3. Board layouts

- **3 × 3 (default full system):** logo cover · construction · digital application ·
  brand essence/tagline · color system · typography · physical application · image
  direction · system detail.
- **2 × 3 (mini deck):** wordmark · product surface · functional panel · atmosphere
  image · symbol/badge · tagline.
- Also: 2 × 2 concept board, 1 × 3 strip, 4 × 2 contact sheet.

Give the board rhythm (quiet, functional, emotional, technical, atmospheric, detailed);
not every panel equally loud. Consistent gutters, clean grid, generous negative space.

## 4. Visual modes (pick by category)

| Mode | For | Cues |
|---|---|---|
| Dark builder | Dev tools, infra, automation | Near-black, monospace accents, terminal/prompt frames, cyan/coral/lime |
| Dark operator | Business, growth, productivity | Black/amber/red, UI chips, progress motifs |
| Dark calm | Strategy, travel, wellness | Deep green + lime, misty imagery, editorial grid |
| Security | Security, compliance, monitoring | Navy/black, shields, radar lines, alert chips |
| Light editorial | Legal, privacy, trust | Ivory paper, serif labels, seals, deep blue/red/gold |
| Luxury / beauty | Fashion, hospitality, premium services | Ivory/stone/espresso, serif wordmark, monogram, emboss |
| Warm local | Food, family business, community services | Warm palette, tactile paper, hand-drawn line accents, real product photography |
| Voice | Voice AI, chat, audio | Indigo/lilac, waveform, mic, pulse |
| Cultural | Music, events, creative tools | Halftone, analog print, bold accent, poster panels |

## 5. Detail, text, color

- Premium detail rewards a closer look: page numbers, thin rules, construction lines,
  one highlighted word, one accent chip. Don't overuse.
- Very little text: brand name, one short specific tagline, 2–5 labels. No paragraphs,
  lorem ipsum, or tiny fake UI.
- One dominant palette (base, primary accent, secondary accent, neutrals); accents repeat
  across panels; no random rainbow or default AI glow.
- Mockups are identity applications (browser bar, app icon, card, label, packaging), not
  feature demos.
- With user references: extract rhythm, grid, spacing, type scale, density, image
  treatment; never copy their logo, name, slogan, or composition.

## 6. Check before showing

Strategy visible in the mark · mark consistent across panels · readable sparse type ·
disciplined palette · board rhythm varied · no generic or copied elements.
