# Direction library

Seeds for explore-design Stage 2. Offer one only when the brief's feel matches it; a direction is a starting point, never a requirement to enlarge the brief. The brief, the project's brand and the "faithful, evolve or explore" answer always win.

**A direction holds only its look.** Every direction builds on the floor ([ui-build](../../../implement/references/ui-build.md) Pillars 1–4 and [page craft](../../../implement/references/ui-build/page-craft.md)) and never overrides it. A direction may override a [web](../web.md) default only by listing each one under an `## Overrides of web.md` heading (for example, gradient hero text against web.md §6). A direction may be stricter than the floor, never looser.

- **[Taste](taste/README.md)**: premium/sophisticated · comfort/warmth · modern. Offer for landing pages, portfolios, marketing sites and redesigns that should look custom rather than templated; it covers the design read, dials, system choice, redesign preservation and a pre-flight check.
- **[Minimalist editorial](minimalist-editorial.md)**: editorial calm. Offer when the owner asks for minimalist, editorial, document-style, Notion/Linear-like, clean or calm (warm monochrome, generous whitespace, flat components, muted pastel accents).
- **[Clean geist](clean-geist.md)**: crisp single-typeface conversion UI. Offer for SaaS, product, service and conversion one-pagers that want a clean Geist-style look (one typeface, flat backgrounds, greyscale gradient heading, tagline reveal, weighted motion).
- **[Industrial brutalist](industrial-brutalist.md)**: raw industrial. Offer when the owner asks for brutalist, industrial, terminal, military/aerospace, blueprint or data-dense raw aesthetics (Swiss grids, extreme type contrast, analog degradation).
